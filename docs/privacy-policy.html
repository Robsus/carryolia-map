Carryolia – Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Carryolia is a strategy game played on the real map, made by Rob van den Bekerom (the Netherlands). This policy explains what happens with your data when you play. In short: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and your games stay on your phone. The game does not use your phone's location. To show the map, it asks a few map services for data about the area you play in.

1. Location

The game does not ask for, use or store your phone's location. You choose where to play by tapping the map.

2. Map services the game talks to

To draw the map and load the buildings, roads, forests and water where you play, the game requests data for the map area you are playing in. These requests go directly from your phone to the following services. Like any web request, they include your IP address and the requested map area.

ServiceWhat forOperator
GitHub (github.com, release downloads)Buildings, roads, nature in the game: pre-built map data files, downloaded in pieces for the area you play inGitHub, Inc. (Microsoft), USA
OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org)Background map imagesOpenFreeMap, via Cloudflare

I have no access to what these services log. Their own privacy policies apply. The game does not send them any identifier other than what every web request contains.

3. Data stored on your phone

Your saved games, settings (music volume, display options) and downloaded map data are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. If the game ever closes because of an error, a short technical note about it is kept there too; it stays on your phone unless you send it with feedback (section 5). If Android backup is on, your saved games and settings may be included in your Google account's device backup (downloaded map data is excluded). Uninstalling the game deletes all of it.

4. Shared gaming (same wifi)

You can play one game together with other phones on the same wifi network. This only happens when you choose to host or join a game. The phones then talk to each other directly over that wifi; nothing goes through the internet or through a server of mine.

  • A phone that hosts a game announces it on the wifi as "Carryolia" with the game's name, so other phones on that network can find it.
  • When phones connect, each one tells the other its model name (for example "Pixel 8") and the game version. Then the host sends the whole game to the guest, which keeps a copy of it, and both phones exchange what the players do while they play.
  • The connection is not encrypted, so only play together on a wifi network you trust.

5. Pictures and feedback you send yourself

The game can make a picture of your empire on the map (with its town, numbers and a link to the game) and open your phone's share menu, and it can open a feedback mail to me, if you like with your saved game and a performance report attached. A saved game and a picture of the map show where on the map you play; the feedback mail and report also include the game version, your phone's model and its Android version. Nothing is sent by the game itself: you choose the app, the people and whether to press send. If you e-mail me, I only receive what you choose to send, and use it only to answer you and to improve the game.

6. What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in, no name or e-mail address asked.
  • No advertising networks, no advertising ID.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

7. Links

Links in the game (for example on its Credits page) open in your phone's browser; the website's own privacy policy applies there.

8. Google Play

The game is installed and updated through Google Play. When it starts, it asks Google Play whether a newer version is available; that check is handled by Google Play on your phone under Google's privacy policy. Google Play may also share crash reports and app performance data with me if you have allowed this in your phone's settings; these do not identify you.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you and to have it removed. Apart from e-mails you sent me yourself, I hold none. Questions: Carryolia@hotmail.com.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
